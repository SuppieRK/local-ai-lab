## Introduction

> You've probably seen those "simple home AI lab" or "build your own agent" guides featuring Mac clusters, Lenovo
> ThinkStation PX boxes, or an RTX 6000 casually mentioned like it's a USB stick.

As an engineer who enjoys learning, tinkering, and occasionally gaming, building a personal AI lab seemed like a
reasonable idea (and a justification to my wife that spending money on that GPU was a career investment 😶).

The problem: AI content overload. Everyone's setup seems to be like the kid my mom always compares me to.

This repository exists to document what actually worked for me – without the hype cycle, affiliate links, or
philosophical essays about "the future of intelligence."

## Goals

- Focus on practical aspects of a home AI lab:
    - Use open-source software (because vendor lock-in for a hobby is unnecessary).
    - Offline capability (to maintain flexibility for a potential transition into a forest-based remote role).
- Straightforward content without fluff (and hopefully as simple as possible).
- Include small Bash snippets where useful. Not a framework. Not a platform. Just scripts.

## Non-goals

- Exhaustive coverage of every possible configuration.
- Being the definitive guide to anything.
- Packaging everything into a one-click NPM abstraction that installs 400 dependencies and calls itself "minimal."

> If it helps you, good. If not, fork it and make it better.
